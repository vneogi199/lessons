# Mission: Become an International Full Stack AI Engineer

## Why
Build the depth, practical judgment, interview skill, and public proof needed to win full-stack AI engineering roles at international companies, including roles that can support relocation. The goal is to ship dependable AI products and explain how they work from browser and runtime internals through distributed systems and model behavior.

## Success looks like
- Pass coding, JavaScript/Node.js, Python, frontend, backend, AI, system-design, and behavioral interview rounds.
- Design, build, test, secure, observe, and deploy production-style full-stack AI systems without relying on unexplained abstractions.
- Explain important behavior behind the scenes, including browser rendering, JavaScript runtimes, Node.js concurrency, databases, networks, distributed systems, transformers, retrieval, agents, and evaluations.
- Present three polished portfolio systems with architecture records, tests, operational evidence, and concise technical communication.
- Collaborate effectively in English across cultures, time zones, code reviews, incidents, and architecture discussions.

## Constraints
- Begin from basic JavaScript and Python familiarity.
- Assume 6–8 focused hours per week, with a 24-week interview core and self-paced deep extensions.
- Prefer code, analogies, high-contrast visuals, blackboard diagrams, retrieval practice, and realistic engineering tasks.
- Keep the roadmap editable so new topics and target-market requirements can be added later.

## Out of scope
- Training frontier foundation models from scratch or preparing for research-scientist roles.
- Memorizing every framework or cloud-vendor product.
- Country-specific immigration or legal advice before target destinations are selected.
