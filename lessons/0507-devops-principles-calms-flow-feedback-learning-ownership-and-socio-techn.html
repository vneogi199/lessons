DevOps principles

DevOps is a socio-technical operating model that improves the flow of changes and feedback across development and operations. Automation supports it but does not create shared ownership by itself.

CALMS

CALMS summarizes culture, automation, lean flow, measurement, and sharing. It is a diagnostic lens rather than a product checklist.

flow

Flow measures how work moves from request to usable delivery, including waiting and handoffs.

feedback

Feedback compares observed behavior with an intended result so the next attempt can be adjusted.

learning

Learning turns incident and delivery evidence into tested changes rather than repeated blame or intuition.

ownership

Ownership assigns responsibility for creating, changing, and releasing state or resources. Clear ownership explains who closes a connection or resolves a conflicting update.

socio-technical systems

Socio-technical systems combine software, people, incentives, and organizational boundaries that jointly affect outcomes.
