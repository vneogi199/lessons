01

Networking

Professional networking builds mutual context and trust through useful, specific, respectful interactions over time rather than requesting favors from strangers at scale.

02

open source

Open-source work provides public evidence of understanding an unfamiliar codebase, scoping changes, testing, review response, documentation, and asynchronous collaboration.

03

referrals

A referral is a person's informed introduction or endorsement. Ask only when they have enough context to judge your fit and make declining easy.

04

communities

Communities provide peer feedback and shared practice; participation should be relevant, respectful, and consistent with your preferences.

05

outreach

Outreach starts a relevant professional conversation with a clear, modest request rather than indiscriminate repeated messaging.

06

international communication

International communication makes time zones, deadlines, decisions, uncertainty, tone, and handoffs explicit so collaboration does not depend on shared local context.
