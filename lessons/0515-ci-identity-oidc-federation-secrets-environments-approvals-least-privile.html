CI identity

CI identity determines which workflow can obtain credentials and act on deployment or artifact resources.

OIDC federation

OIDC federation lets a workflow exchange signed identity claims for short-lived cloud credentials. Trust rules should bind repository, branch, environment, and intended audience.

secrets

Secrets are credentials or sensitive values that grant access, such as tokens and private keys. Limit their scope and lifetime, rotate them, and exclude them from source and logs.

environments

Environments separate configuration, access, data, and release stages; names alone do not enforce isolation.

approvals

Approvals authorize high-impact transitions after evidence review, with accountable identity and audit history.

least privilege

Least privilege grants only the actions and resources needed for a task. It reduces the damage a bug or compromised identity can cause.

runner security

Runner security isolates untrusted jobs, limits credentials, and prevents persistence or cross-job data leakage.

A workflow's identity determines which credentials it can obtain. Short-lived tokens reduce credential lifetime, but untrusted code running with deployment authority can still misuse them. Bind federation to exact provider claims and protected environments. Check the artifact's provenance as well as the workflow identity. Runner isolation must prevent one job from retaining access to another job's data or credentials.
