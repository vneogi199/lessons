AWS cost models

AWS cost models combine service-specific usage dimensions, regional rates, commitments and shared costs. Model uncertainty and cost per successful business outcome rather than request count alone.

tags

AWS tags attach key/value metadata to supported resources for organization, policy or cost allocation. Coverage and activation rules matter; a tag alone is not an isolation boundary.

CUR

AWS Cost and Usage Reports provide detailed billing/usage records for allocation and analysis. Select the supported export/report format and account for reporting delay and adjustments.

Cost Explorer

Cost Explorer analyzes supported historical and forecasted costs using billing dimensions. Forecasts and delayed billing evidence are not real-time hard spending limits.

Budgets

AWS Budgets monitors configured cost or usage thresholds and can trigger configured alerts or actions. Reporting and action delays mean it does not guarantee a strict spending ceiling.

rightsizing

Rightsizing matches provisioned resources to measured workload needs with headroom for failure and variation. Reducing nominal capacity is useful only while service objectives remain satisfied.

Savings Plans

Savings Plans exchange a defined spending commitment for eligible discounted usage. Utilization, coverage and flexibility determine value; unused commitments still cost money.

Spot

Spot capacity uses spare provider capacity under interruption and availability constraints. Design checkpointing, diversification and fallback around the chosen service contract.

FinOps

FinOps coordinates engineering, finance and product ownership of cloud economics. Allocation, forecasting and unit-cost decisions connect spending to business value and service constraints.
