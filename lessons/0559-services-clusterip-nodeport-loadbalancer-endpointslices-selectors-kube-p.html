Kubernetes stores desired state in API objects. Controllers work continuously to make actual state match desired state.

Services

A typical selector-based Service gives stable access to changing Pod endpoints. Headless Services do not allocate a cluster virtual IP; selectorless and ExternalName Services use different backend or DNS mechanisms.

ClusterIP

ClusterIP exposes a Service through a stable cluster-internal virtual address under the cluster networking implementation.

NodePort

NodePort exposes a Service through a configured port on nodes, with reachability governed by networking and security policy.

LoadBalancer

LoadBalancer requests an implementation-managed load balancer for a Service where supported.

EndpointSlices

EndpointSlices hold scalable groups of Service backends and readiness information consumed by the network data plane.

selectors

Selectors match labels to choose resources or endpoints; accidental label matches can route traffic to the wrong Pods.

kube-proxy

kube-proxy implements Service traffic forwarding on many clusters; some networking implementations replace its role.

traffic policies

Service traffic policies influence endpoint locality and forwarding behavior, affecting source addresses and availability.
