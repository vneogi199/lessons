Jobs

Jobs run work toward completion and may create replacement Pods; application effects must tolerate possible repeated execution.

CronJobs

CronJobs schedule Jobs over time with concurrency and missed-run policies, not an exactly-once clock guarantee.

completions

Job completions specify required successful work units under the configured completion mode.

parallelism

Job parallelism bounds concurrently active work, subject to scheduling and controller behavior.

backoff

Backoff spaces repeated attempts to reduce pressure. Bound attempts and total time, and use jitter to avoid synchronized retries.

deadlines

A deadline is the latest acceptable completion time. Each downstream operation should consume the remaining budget rather than starting a fresh full timeout.

concurrency policy

CronJob concurrency policy governs overlapping Jobs from that CronJob, not every external side effect or separately created Job.

failure handling

Failure handling specifies retry, termination, and diagnosis for workload failures rather than relying only on Pod replacement.
