08 · Common mistakes

Memorizing the label

A definition is not enough. Trace an interview scenario from input to output.

Testing only success

The happy path can hide a weak design. Try this failure: Answer one prompt at three lengths and remove every claim that cannot be traced to a real event.

Changing too much

Change one condition at a time. Otherwise, you will not know what caused the result.

Guessing

Check the system instead: Prompt mapping, timing, decisions, personal actions, result evidence, limitation, and reflection.
