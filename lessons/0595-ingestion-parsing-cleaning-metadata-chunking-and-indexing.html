01

Ingestion

Ingestion receives source data and moves it through parsing, cleaning, metadata, chunking, and indexing steps.

02

parsing

Parsing converts a source format into structured text, fields, and document elements.

03

cleaning

Cleaning removes or repairs content that would reduce retrieval quality while preserving important meaning.

04

metadata

Metadata is structured information about content, such as its source, owner, date, language, or access policy.

05

chunking

Chunking divides content into retrieval units that have useful meaning and manageable size.

06

indexing

Indexing builds data structures that let a search system find candidate content efficiently.
